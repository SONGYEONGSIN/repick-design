## c — Strata (Exploded Evidence Stack)

The hero replaces every prior single-widget verdict device with a physically-stacked deck of five bordered "layer" cards — Style Fit, Size, Condition, Price, Trend — that sit visibly overlapped like transparent film sheets on load (opacity always 1, only `translateY`/`scale`/`rotate`/`z-index` change, so the layers are never invisible mid-sequence), then separate downward into their own row via a deterministic `framer-motion` `whileInView` + `staggerChildren` sequence once the hero scrolls into view, before resolving into an always-visible verdict strip (aggregate match %, letter grade, verified-seller line, before/after discount) that fades in last so the sequence reads as collapsed → separated-evidence → resolved-verdict, not a random shuffle. This is a fresh output-visualization archetype distinct from every prior form in the corpus: unlike r5a's radial dial, r6a's certificate document, r6c's estimate receipt, r7b's bar-chart equalizer, or r7c's photo annotation pins, the reveal mechanism here is a spatial deck of overlapping cards physically separating apart (an exploded-diagram metaphor), with no document/seal/receipt skin and no line-art/blueprint decoration (the only "graphic" element is a large ghost numeral "05," matching the DNA's existing ghost-number convention). Manipulation = value realization: clicking any layer, or arrow-keying through a `role="tablist"` of the five layer buttons (roving `tabindex`, Home/End support), pulls that layer forward (`translateX` + scale bump, siblings recede) and swaps an adjacent `role="tabpanel"` to that criterion's full reasoning paragraph and its own 0–100 sub-score — a real content change, not decoration, independent of the one-time scroll-assembly animation. `prefers-reduced-motion` mounts every layer already in its resolved, fully legible position and disables the assembly transform entirely, while the click/keyboard layer-selection and detail-panel swap remain fully interactive either way. The always-visible verdict strip (match %, grade, verified badge, before/after discount) sits structurally outside the stack's animated region so core proof is never gated behind the interaction, per the DNA's "at-rest" requirement. The product-preview section ships four parallel rich cards (coat, tote, skirt, boots), each permanently showing its match-score chip, condition-grade chip, seller-verification line, top matching signal, and before/after discount at rest — with match/grade chips placed in their own row directly under the photo (never overlaid on top of it) so a failed image load can never leave alt text overlapping a badge, addressing the recurring image/badge-overlap defect class. Four distinct interactions total: (1) hero click/keyboard layer pull-forward with real detail-panel content swap, (2) hero scroll-triggered staggered stack-separation sequence, (3) product-preview card hover/focus micro-interaction (image scale + lift) on top of always-visible proof chips, and (4) a deterministic on-scroll count-up of the social-proof stats (`framer-motion` `animate()`, fixed duration/ease, no `Math.random`/`Date.now`/`new Date` anywhere). Copy is English-only, near-monochrome with the single `#6E56CF` accent always present at rest, exactly three font weights (400/600/800 via Tailwind classes only), the three-tier eyebrow/caption/stat tracking scale, and a `<lg`/`lg:` `clamp()` split on both the hero and closing headlines to avoid 375px overflow.
