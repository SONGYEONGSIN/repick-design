## a — Signal Graph

The hero replaces every prior console/dial/document/board metaphor with an explicit node-link relationship diagram: a left cluster of five preference-signal nodes (Style, Size, Budget, Condition, Trend, rendered as an ARIA `role="tablist"` of circular/pill buttons stacked at fixed build-time y-percentages) wired via a fixed-coordinate SVG overlay (twenty cubic-bezier paths, `viewBox="0 0 100 100"` `preserveAspectRatio="none"` so DOM percentage positions and SVG path coordinates stay pixel-exact across breakpoints) to a right cluster of four live matched listings, each permanently showing match %, condition grade, verified-seller badge, and before/after discount at rest — never hover-gated, and the copy never instructs a hover-to-reveal action. Edge stroke-width and opacity encode real per-pair match strength (`EDGE_STRENGTH[pref][product]`, 0-100, hand-authored data, no runtime computation); on mount every wire draws in once via `motion.path` `pathLength` with a deterministic index-driven stagger (`prefIndex*0.11 + productIndex*0.035`, no `Math.random`/`Date.now`/`new Date` anywhere), gated behind `prefers-reduced-motion` (skips straight to final opacity/width, no stuck mid-draw or opacity:0 state). Selecting a preference node — by click, Enter, or roving-tabindex arrow-key navigation (Up/Down/Left/Right/Home/End, one tab-stop, `aria-selected`/`aria-controls`) — is the manipulation-equals-value-realization mechanism required by the brief: it re-weights which of the twenty wires are emphasized (muted-gray/near-invisible for the other four signals' wires vs. accent-purple scaled by strength for the active signal's four wires) AND rewrites an adjacent `aria-live` evidence panel with that dimension's real reasoning sentence (`REASONING[pref]`, tied to the argmax-strength product for that dimension) plus a live five-row strength-bar breakdown across all four products that visibly changes width/value per click — a genuine data recompute, not a decorative replay, satisfying the accumulated "manipulation = value realization" and "hover-copy mismatch" deltas simultaneously. Default state (Style active, coat highlighted) is fully legible with zero interaction, so nothing requires a click to see core value. This is a fresh archetype distinct from every prior form in the corpus (live console, timeline, drag-slider, snap-deck, masonry filter, chat transcript, calculator, swipe stack, tilt-spotlight, compare-table, radial dial, closet-rail, search-chips, certificate, receipt-recompute, split-flap, equalizer bars, annotation pins) — it is the first genuine multi-node relationship/graph visualization, closer in spirit to a Notion/Linear relation view than to any dial, table, or document. Because the concept's proof does not depend on any photo, the hero is pure SVG/CSS/data — no `next/image` in the hero at all — while the lower product-preview section (four parallel rich cards, same four listings continued from the graph) uses `next/image` with `fill` inside `aspect-[4/3]` containers that reserve a fixed background color, and — learning from the r7 alt/badge-overlap risk — moves all proof badges to a bottom scrim strip instead of the top-left corner where a failed image's browser alt-icon/text renders, so a broken remote image can never visually collide with the match%/grade badges. Four total distinct interactions: hero signal-node selection (real edge/evidence recompute), the deterministic edge draw-in sequence, `whileInView` scroll reveals through preview/method/proof/CTA, and product-card hover/focus lift+scale layered on top of the always-visible baseline. Copy is English-only, near-monochrome with the single `#6E56CF` accent present at rest, exactly three font weights (400/600/800 via Tailwind classes, no `next/font`), the three-tier eyebrow/caption/stat tracking scale, and a `<lg`/`lg:` `clamp()` split on both hero and closing headlines to avoid overflow at 375px.
