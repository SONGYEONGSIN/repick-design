## c — AI Annotation Scan

The hero is a single large real product photograph (a vintage wool Chesterfield coat, shot alone against a plain backdrop) overlaid with five real, focusable `<button>` annotation pins — fixed at build-time `%` coordinates, never computed at runtime — each pointing at a specific detail the AI actually examined: fabric weave, brand-tag authenticity, stitching integrity, cuff wear pattern, and color-match confidence. A plain deterministic step counter (`setInterval`, no `Math.random`/`Date.now`/`new Date` anywhere) advances the active pin every 2.8s and loops back to pin 1 on a fixed cycle; clicking or focusing any pin jumps straight to it and hands permanent manual control to the visitor, satisfying the r5 "output-visualization, not input-manipulation" preference (the AI has already computed every finding — the pins let you explore the result, not produce one). Selecting a pin swaps an adjacent evidence panel to that pin's real finding sentence plus its own confidence sub-score, while a "running match" figure beside it counts up via framer-motion's imperative `animate()` through precomputed cumulative averages (98 → 97 → 97 → 95 → 95) as each check is folded in — `tabular-nums`, `aria-live="polite"`, no layout properties animated. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the interval entirely and opens the sequence already on its fully legible final pin/score (all five findings reachable via the same buttons), so nothing is ever caught mid-scan or stuck at `opacity:0`. This is a fresh archetype distinct from every prior form in the corpus: unlike r1a's live-matching console, r5a's radial dial, r6a's certificate document, or b's vertical bar-chart equalizer, the diagnostic device here is annotation pins layered directly onto the actual product photo itself — closer to an image-review/design-QA comment thread than any console, dial, board, or chart. On mobile the evidence panel sits in normal document flow below the annotated image (not an overlay), so narrow viewports read top-to-bottom instead of stacking overlapping layers. The product-preview section ships four parallel rich cards (coat, bag, sneakers, trench coat), each permanently showing its match-score tag, condition grade, verified-seller badge, and before/after discount at rest — never hover/focus-gated — with hover/focus adding a purely additive image-scale + card-lift on top of that baseline, giving four total distinct interactions alongside the hero pin-selection, the score count-up, and `whileInView` scroll reveals used through the method/proof/closing sections. Copy is English-only, near-monochrome with the single `#6E56CF` accent present at rest, exactly three Tailwind font weights (400/600/800, no `next/font` import), the three-tier eyebrow/caption/stat tracking scale, and a `<lg`/`lg:` `clamp()` split on both the hero and closing headlines to avoid overflow at 375px.
