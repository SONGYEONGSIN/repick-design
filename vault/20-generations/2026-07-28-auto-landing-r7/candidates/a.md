## a — Split-Flap Departure Board

The hero replaces static copy with a mechanical Solari/airport-style split-flap board: a live `<table>` of 4 AI-matched listings (item, match %, condition grade, price) rendered as monospace flap cells, each character cycling through a deterministic scramble sequence — a pure function of the target glyph over a fixed alphabet ring (space/A–Z/0–9/`%$.,-+'`), no `Math.random`/`Date.now`/`new Date` anywhere — before settling on its final value, with per-cell delay computed purely from fixed row/column offsets (`cellDelay(row, col) = row·90ms + col·12ms`) producing a left-to-right mechanical "spin-down" sweep; `prefers-reduced-motion` skips straight to final glyphs via a derived render value (no stuck intermediate/opacity-0 state). Four category tabs (Outerwear/Bags/Shoes/Tops) sit above the board and swap which 4 rows are displayed, remounting the table body to replay the flip — a direct instance of the r3 "manipulation = real-time value update" principle applied to genuinely different matched-listing data per tab, not decoration. This is the first mechanical-flip/departure-board metaphor in the corpus — distinct from every prior output-visualization hero (r5a's circular gauge, r6a's certificate document, r6c's estimate calculator) and from every input-manipulation form (sliders, drag rails, swipe stacks, chat logs, search chips): it is neither a dial nor a document but a *live manifest*, closer to r4c's compare-tabs in that tabs swap data, yet visually and structurally unrelated (fixed-width monospace flap cells vs. a comparison table). Accessibility is handled by pairing every flap-cell group with a `sr-only` plain-text equivalent and `aria-hidden` on the decorative glyph spans, plus a real `<table>`/`<caption>`/`<th scope="col">` structure and an `aria-live` row-count announcement on tab change. The product-preview section ships 3 parallel rich cards (coat, bag, sneaker) with match-score tag, condition grade, verified-seller badge, and before/after discount all always visible at rest (never hover/focus-gated), with hover/focus adding a purely additive lift + border-brighten on top of that baseline — the fourth interaction, alongside the hero flip-in, tab-switching, and scroll-triggered `whileInView` reveals used throughout the value-split, social-proof, and closing-CTA sections. Copy is English-only, near-monochrome with the single `#6E56CF` accent present at rest (tab active state, badges, CTA), exactly three font weights (400/600/800 via Tailwind classes only, no `next/font` import), the three-tier tracking scale (eyebrow/caption/stat), and a `<lg`/`lg:` clamp split on the hero and closing headlines to avoid 375px overflow.
