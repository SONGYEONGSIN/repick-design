# Candidate a — Listing Q&A

A buyer-facing public question-and-answer thread for a single listing: a compact, non-interactive item-context header (thumbnail initials + name + price) stays fixed above a scrolling `FlatList` of Q&A entries, each showing the question, asker name, a hardcoded relative timestamp ("2d ago"), a toggleable upvote count, and a nested answer block (from the seller or another buyer) or a "No answer yet" placeholder. The gate "check" render-string used is the screen's accessibility-header text, **"Questions & Answers"**. The bottom band is an always-mounted composer bar (multiline `TextInput` + "Post Question" `Pressable`) rather than a derived blocked/ready workflow band, since the screen has no step-gating flow to represent — it is always ready to accept a new question. The composer's enablement is still a real derived signal: `canPost = useMemo(() => draft.trim().length > 0, [draft])`, recomputed every render, driving both the button's visual disabled style and its `accessibilityState={{ disabled }}`, never a hand-flipped boolean. Posting clears the draft and shows a single live-region confirmation message that self-clears via a cleanup-safe `setTimeout`. Upvoting is a deterministic toggle: each question's upvote state is `{ count, active }` seeded from fixed data in `data.ts`, and tapping flips `active` and increments/decrements `count` accordingly — no `Math.random`, no probabilistic drift.

## Brief gaps

- ① The brief didn't specify how an upvote should visually/semantically distinguish "I upvoted this" from the raw count. ② I modeled it as a two-state toggle (`active` boolean + `count`) with an accent-tinted border/background when active, mirroring a typical "like" affordance rather than a plain incrementing counter. ③ Arbitrary choice, but it keeps the toggle genuinely reversible (tap again to undo) rather than a one-way counter, which felt closer to "deterministic toggle" as the brief explicitly asked for.
- ① The brief didn't specify what happens to the composer or list when a question is successfully "posted" (no backend exists in this mock). ② I decided posting only clears the draft and shows a transient live-region confirmation, without prepending a fake new entry to the FlatList's static data. ③ Avoids implying the mock has write-persistence it doesn't have, and avoids writing an `accessibilityHint` that promises an outcome (like "appears at the top of the thread") the handler doesn't actually deliver.
- ① The brief didn't specify how to label the asker/answerer's identity when the answer isn't from the seller. ② I added a lightweight `responderRole: "seller" | "buyer"` tag rendered inline after the name ("· Seller" / "· Buyer"). ③ General practice for product Q&A sections (e.g. distinguishing an official seller reply from a crowd-sourced buyer answer), and it reinforces that this is public multi-party Q&A rather than a two-party private thread.
