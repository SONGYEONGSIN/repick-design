# Candidate b — Report a Buyer

`ReportBuyerScreen` (`native/src/evolve/r42/b/ReportBuyerScreen.tsx`, data in `native/src/evolve/r42/b/data.ts`) is a seller-facing complaint form filed against a buyer's conduct on a specific past transaction — the mirror direction of reporting a listing, not a reskin of it. The gate "check" render-string is the screen heading, **"Report a Buyer"**. It's a three-step wizard: step 1 is a single-select `FlatList` of four fixed reason rows (`No-show at meetup`, `Never paid`, `Abusive messages`, `Other` — a meetup/payment-behavior taxonomy, deliberately not the listing-content taxonomy another screen in this codebase uses); step 2 is a multiline `TextInput` with a live `{length} / 500` character count and a 10-character minimum; step 3 is a read-only review of the chosen reason and typed details against the reported transaction (fixed buyer/order/listing/date strings from `data.ts`). The bottom gate band is a blocked/ready state machine: on step 1 it reads "Select a reason to continue" until one is picked, on step 2 it counts down "Add at least N more characters to continue" until the minimum is met, and on step 3 it's always ready to submit. Pressing it while ready advances the step (or submits on step 3); every step change and the final submission both route through one `announce()` call that drives the screen's single `accessibilityLiveRegion="polite"` container, with the live text itself carrying `accessibilityRole="alert"`. On submit, the wizard pane and the gate band are both replaced by a local success panel (reference number, reported buyer, reason) with no band underneath it — a finished report has nothing left to gate, so it gets an action-free read-only ending rather than a vestigial second band.

## Brief gaps
- **Decided:** a numeric minimum of 10 trimmed characters for step 2's details field, rather than leaving the "add at least N characters" gate unspecified. **Why:** the brief's own example copy ("Add at least 10 characters to continue") named the number, so I took it as the intended threshold rather than inventing a different one.
- **Decided:** the step-3 submit button uses `tokens.color.danger` while steps 1–2's "Continue" uses `tokens.color.accent`. **Why:** the brief explicitly says to reserve `danger` for "the final submit action or destructive-adjacent moments only" — filing a complaint against another person is exactly that moment, so the accent/danger split marks step 3 as consequential without tinting the rest of the adversarial-topic screen red.
- **Decided:** a small transaction-context card (buyer name/handle, order id, listing title, date — all fixed dummy values) shown on step 1 and repeated on step 3, even though the brief only specified the three wizard steps themselves. **Why:** a report needs to be anchored to *which* transaction and buyer it's about; without it the form would read as reporting a buyer in the abstract rather than this specific past deal, so I added it as read-only context rather than an editable field (nothing in the brief asked for buyer/order selection).
