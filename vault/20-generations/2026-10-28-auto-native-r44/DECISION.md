## LEARN — 1 new L1 delta
Appended to `vault/00-principles/native-deltas-provisional.jsonl`:
> A row-level inline-confirm interaction established for a *destructive* action (confirming removes the row — `sessions/ActiveSessionsScreen.tsx`, `evolve/r35/b/LocalPickupZonesScreen.tsx`) generalizes cleanly to a *non-destructive, persistent* outcome: confirming transitions the row into a third state (distinct from default and pending-confirm) that stays visible with a "Change" affordance to reopen it, rather than disappearing or timing out. A single shared `(activeId, phase)` state variable — not per-row booleans — is the correct implementation, because it both (a) structurally guarantees only one row is ever mid-interaction at a time (no simultaneous pending/confirming rows) and (b) as a direct consequence keeps exactly one `accessibilityLiveRegion` active system-wide without any separate coordination logic.

Generalizes the established "turn the confirming surface itself into Cancel/Confirm" doctrine (GENERATION.md §3, `r13/a` band→`r22/b` row-destructive) to a second, non-destructive row outcome shape — distinct from `auto-native-r43`'s multi-cause-routing delta (that one is about *where* a blocked band's jump goes; this one is about row confirm *outcome persistence*, and applies in a screen with no band at all).
