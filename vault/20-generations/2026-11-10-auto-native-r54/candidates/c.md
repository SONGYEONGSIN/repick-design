# Candidate c — Seller Performance Breakdown

A read-only accordion of 4 fixed seller-score metrics, each toggled open/closed independently via a `Set<string>` of expanded ids (any combination open at once, not a single-open index); the screen's one real action, "Request Manual Review," sits in-flow at the end of the scroll content (no bottom band), is enabled only when a freshly-derived boolean finds at least one metric outside its healthy threshold, always shows a visible reason text whether enabled or disabled, and on press flips the surface into a "Review requested" confirmation instead of a native Alert.

- Sibling data file: `native/src/evolve/r54/c/data.ts`

## Brief gaps

1. **Which 4 metrics and their exact figures/thresholds** — the brief named example metrics but not concrete numbers. Invented: On-Time Shipping Rate 94% (healthy ≥90%), Average Response Time 5.2h (healthy ≤24h), Buyer Rating 4.6/5 (healthy ≥4.5), Cancellation Rate 7.1% (healthy ≤3%) — chosen so exactly one metric (Cancellation Rate) sits outside its threshold, making `canRequestReview` deterministically `true` without hardcoding the boolean itself; the comparison direction (`higherIsBetter`/`lowerIsBetter`) per metric is computed generically in `isMetricHealthy()`, not special-cased per id.

2. **Two-tier vs. three-tier health severity** — the brief's tokens guidance mentions success/warning/danger together, suggesting a possible three-tier (healthy/borderline/unhealthy) scheme. Decided on a binary healthy/"needs attention" scheme (success vs. danger pill) instead, because the brief's actual gating rule is a single threshold comparison ("below its healthy threshold") — adding an invented borderline band with its own arbitrary margin would be a second, undocumented derived rule layered on top of the one the brief specifies, and risked making the enable/disable logic harder to audit at a glance.

3. **Reason text wording when the action is enabled** — the brief only specified the disabled-state reason ("nothing to review" style). For the enabled state, invented reason text naming which metric(s) triggered eligibility (e.g. "Cancellation Rate is outside its healthy range. A specialist will re-check the numbers behind it.") so the always-visible explanation is informative in both states, not just when disabled.

4. **Post-submit confirmation copy and whether it affects the score** — invented a short confirmation body ("Repick's trust and safety team will re-check the metrics above... This does not change your score on its own.") to make clear the action starts a review process rather than silently fixing anything, since the brief said only "flips into a simple confirmation state" without specifying wording.

5. **Container for the metric list** — brief allowed "plain mapped Pressables in a View" for the small fixed list; used a `ScrollView` (not `FlatList`) as the top-level scroll container since the whole screen (header + 4 rows + action area) is one bounded, non-paginated unit, consistent with how `native/GENERATION.md`'s no-band doctrine examples size static content.
6. **"as of" / period label** — invented a fixed string (`"Last 90 days · snapshot from Nov 3, 2026"`) rather than a computed date, per the determinism rule (no argument-less `new Date()`), to make the record read as a point-in-time snapshot like a real statement would.

## Non-duplication check

- **verification/SellerVerificationScreen** (production; nearest neighbor by "accordion for a seller record screen"). That screen is a `FlatList` accordion of 4 *workflow steps* (identity doc, payout, attestations, review-summary) with a single `expandedStep: number | null` (only one step open at a time), gated by a `blocking` derivation that feeds a **fixed bottom band** acting as a state machine (`jumpTo`/`handleSubmit` that scrolls to and expands the first unresolved step, and a terminal `submitted` lock). This candidate differs in every load-bearing way: it is not a workflow (no steps are "incomplete," nothing blocks anything), its expansion state is a `Set<string>` so any number of rows can be open simultaneously (verified against the brief's explicit "not a single which-one-is-open index" requirement), and its one action lives in-flow at the bottom of the scroll content with no fixed band, no `jumpTo`, and no scroll-to-index logic at all.
- **evolve/r37/b/SellerFeeCommissionStatementScreen** (nearest neighbor by domain word "seller ... statement" and by shape — an accordion of read-only rows with a segmented period selector, computed totals, and `expandedIds: ReadonlySet<string>`). This is the closest sibling in raw shape (both are "accordion over a seller record, Set-based expansion, no destructive confirm"), but it has **zero actions of any kind** — no band, no button, nothing to press beyond expand/collapse — which is exactly the gap this round's brief requires filling with a genuine conditional action. This candidate adds the one thing that screen structurally lacks (a derived-boolean-gated "Request Manual Review" with a disabled-reason and a confirmation flip), and uses distinct style-key names throughout (`metricCard`/`metricHeader`/`actionSection`/`confirmationCard` vs. that screen's `saleCard`/`saleRowHeader`/`breakdownPanel`) and a distinct state-variable name (`expandedMetricIds` vs. `expandedIds`) and toggle function name (`toggleMetric` vs. `toggleExpanded`), per the "rename and restructure, don't just lean on the precedent's state shape" directive.
- **evolve/r31/b/SecurityActivityLogScreen** (nearest neighbor by "read-only seller/account record with one real gated action and a live region"). That screen is a **flat, non-accordion** log (rows never expand) with a **persistent two-button bottom band** ("Sign Out Other Devices" / "Report Suspicious Activity"), each gated by its own count-derived condition, and a destructive action that goes through a **Cancel/Confirm inline-row transform** before committing. This candidate differs by being an accordion (rows do expand, via the Set described above), having exactly one action (not two), having no Cancel/Confirm intermediate step (pressing "Request Manual Review" when enabled commits directly to the confirmation state, since requesting a review isn't destructive), and having no fixed band — the action sits in-flow in the scroll content.
- **evolve/r53/c/SizeReferenceScreen** (named in the brief as this round's immediately-preceding bandless read-only screen). That screen is a filterable/searchable/sortable accordion **reference/lookup tool** over a dataset that can grow or shrink under search — search text, category chips, a catalog/alphabetical sort toggle, a metric-unit toggle, a `FlatList`, and an empty state — with **no action anywhere** (truly bandless end to end). This candidate has none of those controls (no search, no filter chips, no sort toggle, no unit toggle, no `FlatList`, no empty state — the metric list is a fixed 4-row `.map()` over a constant array) and, unlike r53/c, does carry exactly one real action per this round's brief requirement; the two screens share only the Set-based expansion primitive itself (a generic data-structure choice the brief directs every candidate toward, not a borrowed architecture), and use non-overlapping variable/style names (`expandedMetricIds`/`metricCard` vs. `expandedGuideIds`/`guideCard`).

Overall: this candidate's combination — fixed (non-filterable) 4-row Set-based accordion, zero-step single conditional action with an always-visible reason in both enabled and disabled states, and an in-flow (non-banded) confirmation flip — does not match any of the four neighbors above on architecture, even though each neighbor shares exactly one surface trait with it (accordion shape, Set-based expansion, a gated action-with-reason, or "no band").
