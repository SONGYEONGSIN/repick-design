## §4 JUDGE — 3-lens blind panel

Screenshots: 4 widths (1280/1440/1920/390) × 4 scroll points (0/35/70/100%) per candidate = 48 frames total, 0 blanks, all visually distinct. Judges were given 4 frames each (`<v>-1440`, `<v>-1440-s35`, `<v>-1440-s100`, `<v>-390`) per the project's frame-budget guidance. 3 independent `general-purpose` Agent-tool subagents, no cross-lens visibility, candidate concept notes withheld from all 3.

### Lens 1 (brief/DNA compliance) — ranking: **b > a > c**
All three pass every checkable DNA rule (English-only, exactly 3 weights, assigned display-font-only-on-headlines, no gradients/serif, `outline-none` never used standalone, determinism). Independently recomputed each candidate's claimed accent-contrast math from its `tokens.ts` hex values: **a and b's numbers matched to 2-3 decimal places; c's concept note had a small linearization arithmetic error (documentation only — the actual code's token usage/tiering still matches the note's correct pass/fail conclusions)**. Ranking decided on hero-fold margin and rule-3 (live-state-to-CTA) rigor: b's hero ends with by far the largest fold margin of the three (explicit before/after height-budget reasoning in its own concept note explains why it built a compact `HeroProofCard` instead of reusing the full product card) and its `ClosingCTA` calls the *identical* `weekAt(weekIndex)` function `PlayheadScrub` itself calls — the most airtight single-source-of-truth implementation of the three. a clears the fold rule comfortably but with less margin (single-column layout, least space-efficient of the three). c's hero-fold content is fully visible at the required 1440×900 frame but with the tightest margin of the three — flagged as a risk at shorter real-world desktop heights, though not a rule-2 failure at the frame actually checked.

### Lens 2 (commercial completeness, Linear/Stripe/Vercel bar) — ranking: **b > a > c**
Hand-traced each candidate's core derived-state math against its screenshots; all three computed exactly correct values for their default states, with honest edge-case handling (a: genuine 0-result state, no fabricated numbers). Decisive finding — real, hand-traceable bugs on two of the three:
- **a**: no header/nav and no footer anywhere in any of the 4 frames at any scroll position — reads as an unfinished component demo rather than a shipped page, the single biggest completeness gap found.
- **b**: a real but minor off-by-one — `periodLabel(0)` computes "11 weeks ago" for the oldest week, but the track's own static end-caption and the always-visible delta line both hardcode "12 weeks ago" as a literal string rather than calling that function, so dragging the playhead to the far-left edge produces a page that contradicts itself. Minor, edge-of-interaction only, doesn't touch the default state or the trust/verification narrative — and this lens still ranked b 1st, judging it the least damaging of the three found defects.
- **c**: the most damaging finding of the round — `harbor-exchange` is explicitly `verified: false` in its own data, yet `coverageFor()` never filters by `verified` before the UI hardcodes the word "verified" onto the count, so the default-visible hero stat ("4 verified points near Union Yard"), the product-preview coverage line, and the closing CTA headline all repeat the same wrong claim — while `harbor-exchange`'s own detail panel, one click away, correctly shows "Verification pending," directly contradicting the headline. This is visible at default state (zero interaction needed) and repeated in three separate places — a trust-claim bug, not a cosmetic one, on a page whose whole category is a *resale marketplace trust* device.

### Lens 3 (archetype differentiation) — ranking: **a > c > b**
Full side-by-side reads of each candidate's named nearest-neighbor(s), not just domain-word comparison.
- **a**: exhaustive icon/pattern grep across the full v6-v26 + r23-r48 catalog found only 2 near-misses (`r37/a/SearchCombobox.tsx`, `r31/a/RegionMap.tsx`), both read in full and confirmed to be "filter-the-picker-then-commit" devices whose visible output only changes on an explicit select — not a live-query-drives-output device like a. Confirmed genuinely unprecedented input-axis in this catalog — the strongest, most rigorously self-verified novelty claim of the three.
- **c**: its named neighbor (`r27/b/demand-map.tsx`, read in full) is a multi-select subset-toggle driving an aggregate area-proportional treemap — no individual point, no per-item detail panel. c's single-selection-among-7 + live detail-panel + radius-coverage-stat is a confirmed, different reachable-state shape.
- **b**: both named neighbors (`v9/AnnotationScan.tsx` — auto-advancing with discrete click-to-override; `r48/c/TimeWindowSelector.tsx` — a genuine two-handle range) were read in full and confirmed topologically distinct from b's single continuous user-dragged point. But this lens judged the underlying primitive (a native range slider driving a chart+stats) as sitting closest to "a skinned/retargeted slider" relative to a's and c's input-axis novelty — the narrowest differentiation margin of the three, even though no exact prior instance collides.
