## LEARN — 1 new L1 delta
Appended to `vault/00-principles/dash-deltas-provisional.jsonl`:
> When a dashboard macro-layout bucket (nav sidebar + narrow watchlist-style rail + center dominant-chart + right detail/feed pane) has already recurred independently across multiple prior rounds in a catalog — confirmed here at 4 occurrences (`r20/b`, `r29/a`, `r35/a`, `r51/b`), all finance/market-adjacent domains on the identical skeleton — a candidate sharing the same outer region-count and nav/rail/center/detail arrangement can still register as genuinely differentiated if it changes the **content-dominance type** of its center region, specifically by making the center feed/list-dominant with zero chart as its visual center of gravity (any chart demoted to a small supporting element in a side rail, not removed from the page). `auto-dash-r51/c` ("Siftline") did this — rail/center/detail region-count unchanged from the saturated bucket, but implemented with a proportional CSS-grid system (not the saturated bucket's fixed-width flex panes), normal page scroll (not independent-pane scroll), and a chart-free `FeedColumn` as the center. Region-count and nav/rail placement similarity alone is NOT sufficient differentiation (that's exactly what makes the bucket count as saturated); content-dominance type is the axis this round's judging actually rewarded.

Generalizes `dash-brief-v3`'s "레이아웃 아키타입 다양화" rule from "which named macro bucket" (hero / master-detail / 3-pane-market / feed / calendar-board) to a finer-grained axis *within* the already-named "3-페인 마켓" bucket itself — same region count can still collide or differentiate depending on what fills the center.
