### Lens 2 — Commercial completeness: **winner**
Entire data layer is generative and reconciled from a seeded LCG, not hand-picked display numbers — risk score is a real weighted formula, calendar intensity buckets are computed against real per-metric domains (including a dynamically-computed revenue max), month-over-month KPI deltas are re-derived from real prior-month records rather than stored as static deltas. CSV export builds a real file from the selected day's actual rows. Mobile (390px) shows genuine layout adaptation (hamburger nav, 2-column KPI grid, stacked calendar/detail), not a squeezed desktop layout.

### Lens 3 — Screen-type differentiation: **winner**
The calendar does real structural work beyond "a calendar exists": a working per-metric bucket/domain calculation, a hover-preview → pin-to-compare interaction pair with no obvious equivalent in the feed/master-detail/hub-spoke shells used in recent rounds, and an AA-safe risk-dimming treatment engineered specifically not to break the heatmap's color-coding contract. Weakest pieces flagged: the KPI row's own structural distinctiveness rests entirely on its data linkage (not on any novel interaction), and the trend-chart card doesn't sync to the selected day, making it the most generic, most swappable single piece on the page — but neither undermines the calendar's own dominance.
