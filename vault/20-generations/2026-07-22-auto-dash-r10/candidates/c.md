## c — Quorum

Customer-success and renewal-health console (Gainsight/Vitally-class) for a CS manager's daily book-of-business review. Hero-number + quadrant-scatter archetype — a light app shell (indigo accent, white/zinc-50 canvas) opens on a single dominant "At-Risk ARR" figure with an inline trend sparkline and three inline supporting stats (accounts at risk, renewals within 60 days, average health score), never a 4-card KPI row. The dominant visualization is a health-score × ARR quadrant scatter: 26 fictional accounts plotted with always-visible axis ticks, dashed quadrant boundary lines at health 60 / $150K ARR, and persistent name labels on the highest-ARR At Risk accounts so risk is legible without hovering. Below it, a sortable/filterable accounts table sits beside an account detail rail (health trend, renewal date, ARR, CSM owner, and a nested sortable/filterable table of that account's recent support and usage signals). Interactions: (1) hover/focus crosshair tooltip on scatter points, (2) click a point to sync the detail rail, (3) click a quadrant label to filter the accounts table to that quadrant, (4) a this-quarter / trailing-12mo toggle that reflows the hero sparkline and re-plots the scatter against a different deterministic snapshot, and (5) a ⌘K command palette to jump straight to an account.
