# Candidate a — Stagebook (reassigned from `auto-dash-r62/a`, queue item #23)

Stagebook is a booking-calendar console for a boutique event-venue operator (Lumen Hall & Studios, four rooms), dominated by a full month grid where every day cell shows its booked revenue and deposit-risk status at a glance, with a Month/Week/Agenda view toggle, a synced day-detail + revenue-trend rail, and a sortable/filterable bookings table whose rows highlight the selected day. Five real interactions carried over unchanged: the view-toggle segmented control, keyboard-reachable sort on the bookings table (`aria-sort`), status + text filtering on that table, day-selection syncing the detail panel and table-row highlight, and a hover/focus crosshair tooltip on the revenue sparkline — plus a ⌘K command palette as the bonus sixth. Confirmed unchanged: light theme on a pure white/zinc-50 canvas (no cream/paper), single AA-corrected `orange-700` accent (orange-600 measured 3.56:1 on white and fails, so every text/solid-fill usage is 700 at 5.18:1), and `--font-display-wide` used only for the "Stagebook" wordmark and the "November 2026" headline. The only functional change from `r62/a` is the hydration-safety fix to the compact-currency formatter (see below), plus wiring three previously-decorative app-shell controls (workspace switcher, notification bell, "New booking") to real state.

## 브리프에 없던 것
1. **Decision**: which calendar-adjacent domain to invent, since the brief only said "calendar- or board-centric, any ops domain." → **Picked**: an event-venue booking calendar (Lumen Hall & Studios), not field-service/dispatch or logistics. → **Why**: this catalog already has a field-service dispatch calendar+board ("Crewline", `/dash/d46`) and a warehouse capacity heatmap calendar ("Openhour", r28/a) — both calendar-dominant and the latter already light+orange. Event-venue booking is a distinct day-to-day domain with its own visual grammar (per-booking revenue chips, deposit status, room occupancy) rather than a near-repeat of either.
2. **Decision**: exact dummy numbers, brand/client names, and the specific November 2026 weekday layout. → **Picked**: November 1, 2026 verified by hand as a Sunday (carried forward from Jan 1 2026 = Thursday, cross-checked against Thanksgiving 2026 landing on Nov 26), so the 35-cell grid needed zero leading padding; 38 hand-authored bookings across ~20 days, revenue/guest counts invented freelancer-brief-style, one cancelled booking, five pending-deposit bookings. → **Why**: arbitrary but internally consistent — every on-screen total (day revenue, month revenue, room occupancy, status counts) is computed from this one `BOOKINGS` array via `getDayStats`/`getMonthStats`/`getRoomUtilization`, never hand-typed twice, so subtotals can't drift from the source rows.
3. **Decision**: how to avoid any horizontal-scroll risk on the bookings table at 390px, since the brief flags `overflow-x-auto` + `table-fixed`/colgroup as the standard fix but also warns against two scrollers on one page. → **Picked**: skip `overflow-x-auto` entirely — desktop renders a real `table-fixed` + `<colgroup>` percentage table (`sm:block`), and mobile (`sm:hidden`) renders the same filtered/sorted rows as stacked cards instead of a scrollable table. → **Why**: this sidesteps the "two wide overflow-x-auto containers" and the `sr-only`-anchor-inside-a-scroller traps completely, rather than relying on precise column-width arithmetic to avoid triggering a scrollbar.
4. **Decision**: status-badge color scheme, since the brief caps the page at one accent (orange). → **Picked**: `emerald-700` for "Confirmed" (conventional success), the single `orange-700` accent itself for "Deposit pending" (framed as the accent's own "needs attention" meaning, not a second brand color), and a dashed neutral `zinc` treatment for "Cancelled." → **Why**: mirrors this catalog's existing "one brand accent + one conventional status complement" convention (e.g. Portlane's emerald accent + rose conflict marker) rather than introducing an unrelated amber/yellow hue that would visually compete with orange as a second accent.
5. **Decision (new, this round)**: how to fix the `trailingZeroDisplay` hydration defect without reintroducing a magnitude-based branch, and whether to also fix the two unwired app-shell controls the reassignment brief flagged as a cross-round finding (workspace-switcher chevron, notification bell), plus a third one found during re-audit ("New booking" primary button). → **Picked**: removed `formatRevenueCompact`'s old `n < 1000 ? usdFmt : usdCompactFmt` branch entirely and replaced it with a single formatter, `usdCompactFmt`, that always runs with `notation: "compact", maximumFractionDigits: 1, trailingZeroDisplay: "stripIfInteger"` explicitly set — no size check anywhere. Separately, wired the workspace switcher to a real listbox popover that switches the displayed workspace, the bell to a real popover listing the (derived, not hand-typed) pending-deposit bookings with a matching badge count, the sidebar-footer user button to a real account menu, and the topbar's "New booking" button to a real modal form that, on submit, jumps the shared `selectedDay` state to the chosen date. → **Why**: the diagnosis in the reassignment brief was explicit that the magnitude branch was the original's failed fix attempt and must not recur, since the defect is implementation/ICU-version-dependent, not value-size-dependent; and the brief's Step 4 explicitly called out the two historical unwired-control findings, so leaving any clearly-interactive-looking control (hover states, chevrons, badges) without a real handler would repeat exactly the kind of defect this reassignment exists to close out.
