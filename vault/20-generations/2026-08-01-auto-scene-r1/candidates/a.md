# a — KEPT (sneaker)

Four states on one fixed WebGL2 layer, driven by document scroll: a three-armed dust vortex → three tilted orbital rings (the sustained dispersed state the manifesto copy reads through) → a side-profile sneaker traced from paths and sampled boundary-shell-first → the wordmark KEPT. Teal-cyan rim palette over a mostly achromatic core on `#010102`, 18,000 particles in one draw call, DPR capped at 1.75. The transition is a **swirl**, not a burst — inner particles rotate further than outer ones, with the angle squared so it is flat at both ends and every state parks unrotated; the ring sprite also widens as the field closes, so dust and orbits read as hollow circles and the two silhouettes read as solid matter. Distinct from motion-pilot in shape (a manufactured object, not a garment/hand/face), in handoff (rotational unwind instead of radial explode-and-regather), in sprite (rings/dots instead of triangle outlines) and in composition (the wordmark ends centred rather than parked to one side).
