# b — Second (wristwatch)

One fixed WebGL2 field (16,000 point sprites, one draw call) is the spine of the whole document and morphs through four states as the page scrolls: **dust** (a spiral of grain with the wake of a hand swept through it) → **orbit** (four concentric tracks with the twelve hour stations, hollow in the middle so the manifesto reads between them) → **dial** (an analog wristwatch — case, chapter ring, sixty-tick minute track, twelve markers, three hands at 10:09, crown and strap, rasterised as linework at 512 and sampled uniformly) → **mark** (the wordmark SECOND). Brand is a resale market for pre-owned goods whose pun is the shape: every object gets a second hand; rose through rose-gold accent on near-black, display type in `--font-display-mono`, three weights.

What separates it from motion-pilot: the transition signature is a **rotational sweep** (particles ride an arc around the centre, further the further out they sit) rather than a radial burst; the sprite is a hairline ring with a pixel-sized centre pip rather than a triangle outline; scroll→morph is piecewise-linear through the capture pipeline's own offsets (0, 0.35, 0.7, 1) so every judged frame lands on a settled state; and size is driven by a per-state openness table instead of a curve fitted to three stages. Rotation is `sin(morph·PI)`, which returns to zero at all four states; particle lag is 0.10, scaled down for four stages. One clock (idle drift), zero under `__SPECIMEN_FREEZE__` and reduced motion with the rAF loop stopped; entry bloom and the 1.5s curtain are mount transitions that are absent/finished when frozen. Measured: a11y 100, freeze and reduced-motion captures byte-identical, idle frame moves without input, no overflow 390–1920.
