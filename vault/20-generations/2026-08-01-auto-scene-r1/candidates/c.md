# c — Reframe (camera)

Reframe is an AI resale-matching service for secondhand objects, and the scene is its lens: one fixed WebGL2 layer morphs continuously through four states against document scroll progress — an eight-bladed **iris diaphragm** (line-drawn, f-stop scale and pinwheel blade edges), a sustained dispersed field of **seven tilted orbit rings** with an open centre the manifesto reads through, a deliberately exaggerated **rangefinder camera** (oversized lens barrel breaking the body rectangle, squared finder hump, cut rangefinder window and aperture pupil), and finally the **REFRAME wordmark**. Transitions are a *shutter twist* rather than a burst: mid-transition every particle rotates about the optical axis by an amount that falls off with radius, so the field winds and unwinds like blades turning and settles exactly at each stage. 14,000 point sprites in a single draw call, rendered as **hexagonal bokeh discs** (shared orientation, bright rim and dimmer core on the large ones) in a sky/cyan rim palette over near-black `#010102`, with a 3.8% ambient dust layer on its own linear size curve; the pointer does a **focus pull** — particles inside the radius push outward, shrink and brighten, opening a lit void the way a subject snaps into focus. No display typeface at all: everything is the house body face at three weights, so the scene carries the whole voice. Distinct from motion-pilot in shape (photographic hardware, no garment/hand/profile), in transition mechanic (twist, not radial burst), in sprite geometry (bokeh hexagons, not hollow triangles), in palette (cyan/sky, not warm gold), and in pointer behaviour (focus pull, not orbit + local zoom lens).
