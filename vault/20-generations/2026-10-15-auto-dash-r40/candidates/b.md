# Candidate b — Calibre

**Concept**: Calibre is a supplier-intelligence console for procurement teams — a radar/spider chart comparing three precision-fastener suppliers across six fixed evaluation axes (Price, Lead Time, Quality, Reliability, Support SLA, Sustainability), with a pin-one-supplier workflow for deciding who gets the next contract.

**Interactions implemented** (5, all `use client`, deterministic, `motion-reduce`-gated):
1. **Crosshair hover tooltip on the chart**: each axis spoke has an invisible hotzone (pointer + keyboard focus) that pops a small info panel listing all three suppliers' exact values for that axis, positioned via the same percentage coordinates used to place the SVG label — no cursor-tracking math.
2. **Pin (persistent) + hover (ephemeral) on the radar itself and on the legend chips**: clicking a vendor chip or the polygon/markers pins it (`aria-pressed`, visible Pin badge, rose highlight + solid stroke); hovering or keyboard-focusing a different one shows a lighter rose preview outline. The preview is explicitly skipped when `hoveredId === pinnedId` (see `RadarChart`'s `effectiveHoverId`).
3. **Real table sort**: clicking any axis or the Composite column header toggles `aria-sort` asc/desc and reorders the three supplier rows; the radar and stat strip are untouched by this (table-only effect).
4. **Raw Score / Percentile Rank view toggle** (segmented control): remaps every number on the page — radar polygon shape, stat strip, legend composite, table, drawer — through a fixed industry-benchmark linear remap (`percentileRank`), no randomness.
5. **⌘K command palette**: global `Cmd/Ctrl+K` listener, lists "Pin \<supplier\>", "Switch to \<view\>", and "Open full scorecard" as filterable, arrow-key-navigable commands.

Plus an on-demand **dismissible scorecard drawer** (nullable, never a permanent third pane) with a per-axis progress-bar breakdown and a generated-SVG sparkline of the pinned supplier's 6-quarter composite trend — opened only via an explicit button or palette command, closed by Escape/backdrop/✕.

**Font/typo confirmation**: body and all copy on `--font-sans` (Pretendard, global default, not set explicitly). `--font-display-wide` is used only for the "Calibre" wordmark and the drawer's supplier-name heading — Latin, headline-scale text, nowhere else. Exactly 3 rendered font weights across the whole route: `font-normal` (400), `font-medium` (500), `font-semibold` (600) — verified by grep, no `font-bold`/`font-light`/etc. anywhere, and every `<th>`/heading that would otherwise inherit browser-default bold has an explicit weight class.

**Chart/value hierarchy check**: the stat strip's per-axis numbers for the pinned supplier (`text-3xl`, 30px) are the largest numerals on the page by design — the composite badges (table `text-sm`/`text-base`, drawer `text-2xl`, legend `text-sm`) and the thin context strip (`text-xs`) are all deliberately smaller, so the radar's own readout stays visually dominant.

**Contrast self-check**: small-text accent links/labels use `rose-700` (not `rose-600`, which is borderline AA at body size) — e.g. "View full scorecard", the tooltip's "(pinned)" tag. Secondary/caption text is `zinc-500` only on white/`zinc-50` surfaces and bumped to `zinc-600` on any `zinc-100`/`rose-50` tinted surface (segmented-control track, status pills, tab rail, pinned-row backgrounds).

## 브리프에 없던 것

- **The product/domain and all vendor data.** Brief offered vendor evaluation as one of three example domains with example axis names, but no entities or numbers. Based on: picked vendor evaluation (fits "console" framing best of the three options) and hand-picked three precision-fastener suppliers with scores that keep each one's strengths/weaknesses legible on the radar (Meridian = balanced/quality-led, Atlas = cheap/fast but weaker quality, Kestrel = sustainability + reliability leader) rather than randomized-looking numbers.
- **The Percentile Rank view's benchmark bands.** Brief never specified a second "view" at all — it's an invented 5th interaction. Based on: wanted a second genuinely different numeric lens (not just a cosmetic toggle) that still reads as deterministic and realistic, so each axis got a fixed industry 10th/90th-percentile reference band and raw scores are linearly remapped against it, never against the other two suppliers (which would trivialize to rank order at n=3).
- **Tabs placement for "Legend & Weighting."** Completeness bar requires a Tabs component but the brief's shell spec didn't say where one goes. Based on: folded it into the chart's secondary panel as "Suppliers" (the pin/hover legend) vs. "Axis Weights" (the composite formula, shown as weighted progress bars) — two genuinely different views of the same card rather than a decorative tab bar bolted on elsewhere.
- **Composite-score weights and formula.** Brief said "comparing entities across fixed attributes" but gave no scoring method. Based on: assigned weights that sum to exactly 1 (Price 20 / Lead Time 15 / Quality 25 / Reliability 20 / Support 10 / Sustainability 10), reflecting a plausible procurement priority order (quality and price weighted highest), and exposed the weights themselves in the UI so the composite number is auditable, not a black box.
