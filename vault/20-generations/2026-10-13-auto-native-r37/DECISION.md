### Lens 1 — DNA / profile compliance
**Ranking: c > a > b.** All three clean on hard-fail checks (RN idioms, token-only colors, determinism, `accessible={true}` trap, destructive-confirm, honest hints — verified by direct grep + full-file read, not assumed). Differentiated on rule-engagement depth and verifiability: c's selection-bar form satisfies every sub-clause of the hardest band rule with cited line evidence (derived `canCreateBundle` not a toggle; single live region with alert only on the transition sentence; clearing timer with unmount cleanup; crossing-only announcement via `wasCreatableRef`). a is clean but engages less rule-surface (correctly argued no-band). b is clean but its "no band" call is the most debatable of the three — GENERATION.md's own worked example for this screen category (read-only financial record) is an always-visible action bar with a real action (r12/c precedent), and a sibling catalog screen ("tax export tagging") suggests export/download is a plausible real affordance b chose to omit entirely rather than surface in a band. Not a violation, but the shakiest of the three band calls to fully defend.

### Lens 2 — mobile app production polish
**Ranking: b > a > c.** Hand-verified b's fee arithmetic pixel-for-pixel against the screenshot (three row nets + sum = summary total, exactly, via pure `computeFeeBreakdown`/`sumNetPayout`) and judged its hero number ("KRW 175,640") both highest-on-page and visually dominant — the strongest "reads like a real seller-finance screen" case of the three. a's primary number (KRW 45,000 earned) is correctly placed high but visually under-weighted, styled identically to two secondary counters rather than dominant. c is DNA-correct but, under this specific "does it look like it belongs next to eBay/Depop" lens, its 8-tile grid of flat gray/zinc placeholder swatches (a sanctioned DNA convention, not a violation) is the most visually placeholder-like thing in the round — though the lens separately confirmed c's apparently-small 24×24 checkbox glyph is cosmetic-only, sitting atop a full-tile `Pressable` hit target, so there's no actual touch-target problem once traced to source.

### Lens 3 — screen-type differentiation
**Ranking: c > b > a.** c is the only candidate to break the macro-layout: a 2-column selectable tile grid is structurally distinct from a/b's shared "header block + vertical FlatList of cards, no band" skeleton. Lens flagged a and b's independent convergence on that identical macro-skeleton — different domains, different interaction nuance (a: filter chips; b: segmented dataset-switch + per-row disclosure), but the same list-with-header shape — as exactly the kind of "safety collapse" the brief asked it to watch for. For c's band specifically, the lens could not do a byte-level diff against the `r16/b` precedent (not in its file set) but found the style-key names (`quoteBar`/`quoteStatusLine`/`quoteCreateButton`, domain-specific discount-tier copy, dual-direction threshold-crossing announcer tied to live discount math) read as freshly written for the bundle-building domain rather than a literal reskin — the opposite of the `r18/c` style-key-collision failure mode GENERATION.md warns against.

Both lenses, without seeing each other's output, separately flagged the same pattern: **a and b's independent "no band" choices converged on the same vertical-list-with-header macro-skeleton despite being assigned different domains and different secondary interaction mechanics.** Lens 1 also independently flagged b's "no band" as DNA-correct-but-debatable.
