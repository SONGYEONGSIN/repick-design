## Per-candidate detail

### a
**Mechanic/domain.** `native/src/evolve/r47/a/InstantTradeInScreen.tsx` — a blocked-workflow state machine (GENERATION.md §3 family) over condition-questionnaire answers for a marketplace-operated instant cash buyback. Phases: `blocked` (jump to first unanswered question via `FlatList.scrollToIndex` + `AccessibilityInfo.setAccessibilityFocus`) → `primed` (all answered, offer computed but deliberately hidden until "See my offer" is tapped) → `offered` (band becomes an Accept/Decline button pair) → `accepted`/`declined` (terminal, with a "Start over" link).

**Neighbors checked.**
- `native/src/evolve/r25/a/TradeInAppraisalScreen.tsx` — the obvious domain neighbor (also trade-in, also a blocked-workflow band), and the one the candidate's own header comment (lines 1-32) explicitly disclaims against. Verified for real: r25/a uses `ScrollView` + `onLayout`/`checkpointY`/`scrollTo` (no `FlatList`, no programmatic a11y focus at all), two-choice Yes/No disclosure buttons (not a 3-option radiogroup), a credit **range** that's visible from the start and merely narrows, and style keys `dockedBar*`/`holdUp`/`revealCheckpoint` vs candidate a's `offerBand*`/`firstUnanswered`/`jumpToQuestion`. Zero shared identifiers or style-key names. The "primed" reveal-gated phase (amount hidden until an explicit tap) does not exist in r25/a at all — a genuine mechanical addition, not a renamed copy.
- `native/src/evolve/r34/a/IdentityReverificationScreen.tsx` — not named in a's own commentary, but the closer **architectural** match for the specific "jump to the blocked FlatList row and move a11y focus onto it" sub-mechanism: both keep a `useRef<FlatList>` (`listRef`, both files) plus a per-row node-ref map (`rowRefs: useRef<Record<string, View|null>>` at a:121-122 vs `stepNodeRefs: useRef(new Map<string,View|null>())` at r34/a:53-54), both resolve the node with `findNodeHandle` and call `AccessibilityInfo.setAccessibilityFocus` inside a `setTimeout` (a:140-149 `jumpToQuestion`, delay `FOCUS_DELAY_MS` vs r34/a:79-105 `jumpToNextStep`, delay `FOCUS_SETTLE_DELAY_MS`), and both use the same `onScrollToIndexFailed` → `scrollToOffset({offset: info.averageItemLength * info.index})` fallback (a:263-268 vs r34/a:192-196 — though this exact fallback also appears in `native/src/pickup/ShipmentPickupScreen.tsx` and `r46/a/ShippingLabelScreen.tsx`, so it reads as a known catalog convention rather than a-to-r34/a-specific copying). No shared style-key names, no shared prose, no shared variable names beyond the generic `listRef`. Given findNodeHandle+setAccessibilityFocus-after-a-delay is close to the only idiomatic way to do programmatic a11y focus in RN, I read this as convergent implementation of a shared library pattern rather than code-surface copying — but it is worth naming since a's own commentary omits this real neighbor.

**Verdict:** clean at the level this lens actually polices (style-key sets, hint copy, comment prose) against its self-declared nearest neighbor r25/a. The r34/a architectural echo is real but narrow and not disqualifying on its own.

### b
**Mechanic/domain.** `native/src/evolve/r47/b/StoreCreditTopUpScreen.tsx` — a single-nullable-id selection-derived band for prepaying into in-app store credit (money moving IN, before any purchase). `selectedPackage = findCreditPackage(selectedPackageId)`; the band is literally `{selectedPackage && <View style={styles.creditBand}...>}` (lines 168-169), then a local `step` ("select"/"confirm") swaps the band's sentence/buttons in place.

**Neighbor checked.** `native/src/evolve/r42/c/SplitPayPlanSelectorScreen.tsx` — same single-nullable-id family, and an even closer domain match than it first appears (both are a pre-purchase payment-selection screen with a fixed small set of options). Comparing directly:
- Candidate b's header comment (lines 21-25): *"There is no separate `showBand` flag anywhere that gets imperatively flipped — `selectedPackage` IS `null` until a tile is tapped, so the band simply has nothing to render until then."*
- r42/c's header comment (lines 19-22): *"`hasSelection` is recomputed from `selectedPlanId` on every render and used directly in JSX to decide whether `<PlanBand>` mounts at all. There is no separately-tracked `showBand` flag that gets imperatively flipped anywhere in this file."* — and again at r42/c:180-181, *"Derived every render from the single piece of selection state. Never a hand-flipped flag."*

The literal phrase "no separate(ly-tracked) `showBand` flag ... gets imperatively flipped" appears in both files' own commentary, essentially unchanged. The underlying architecture matches too: `selectedPlanId|null` / `selectedPackageId|null` driving band presence directly; a second boolean (`confirmed` in r42/c, `step` in b) gating a "confirmed" sub-state that swaps the band into a review layout with a way back to selection (`onChangePlan` / `handleChangePackage`); picking a different option after confirming invalidates the confirmation in both (r42/c:188-196 `if (prev !== id) setConfirmed(false)`; b's `step` is simply reset by the explicit "Change package" press rather than re-selection, since tiles are hidden during `step==="confirm"` — this one piece *is* a real structural difference, so it isn't a 1:1 clone).

Style keys do **not** collide: b uses `creditBand`/`creditBandSentence`/`proceedButton`/`changeButton`/`confirmButtonRow` vs r42/c's `band`/`bandHeadline`/`bandSubtext`/`bandButtonPrimary`/`bandButtonSecondary` — no shared key names, so b avoided the single most heavily-weighted signal in GENERATION.md §3. But the near-verbatim architecture commentary is a real, concrete collision worth flagging.

**Verdict:** a genuine but partial collision — architecture and self-description mirror r42/c closely even though identifiers and style keys were renamed; domain (store-credit top-up vs split-pay financing) is legitimately distinct.

### c
**Mechanic/domain.** `native/src/evolve/r47/c/PointsRedemptionScreen.tsx` + `RewardRow.tsx` — an always-visible points-balance bar above a `FlatList`, with each reward row applying this catalog's row-level inline-confirm-for-irreversible-action idiom (`idle` → `confirm` → `done`, in place, no native Alert).

**Neighbor checked.** `native/src/sessions/ActiveSessionsScreen.tsx` + `native/src/sessions/SessionRow.tsx` — same row-confirm family, and the match is extremely close on every axis:
- **State shape, near-identical:** `activeRewardId/rowPhase/ackTimer` (c:46-48) vs `activeId/activeStatus/removalTimer` (ActiveSessions:52-56); `clearAckTimer` (c:50-55) vs `clearRemovalTimer` (ActiveSessions:58-63) — both the exact same two-line body (`if (X.current !== null) { clearTimeout(X.current); X.current = null; }`); both wired via `useEffect(() => clearXTimer, [])` (c:57 vs ActiveSessions:65).
- **Handler trio, identical shape:** `requestRedeem`/`requestSignOut` both guard with `if (activeId !== null && activeId !== id && <prior phase === terminal>) { finalize prior row }` before taking over the single active slot (c:64-76 vs ActiveSessions:67-80); `cancelRedeem`/`cancelSignOut` both do exactly `setActiveId(null); setActivePhase(null)` (c:78-81 vs ActiveSessions:82-85); `confirmRedeem`/`confirmSignOut` both set the terminal phase, clear then re-arm the same timer ref, and null the active id inside the timeout callback (c:83-94 vs ActiveSessions:87-96); `phaseFor`/`rowStatus` are the identical ternary `activeId === id && phase ? phase : "idle"` (c:96-97 vs ActiveSessions:98-99).
- **Comment-level near-duplication:** c:59-63 — *"Only one row may be non-idle (confirming OR showing its redeemed acknowledgement) at a time, so exactly one accessibilityLiveRegion is ever live on this screen. If a different row's acknowledgement is still showing when a new 'Redeem' is tapped, finalize that previous one right away..."* vs ActiveSessions:67-71 — *"Only one row may be non-idle (confirming OR mid sign-out) at a time, so exactly one accessibilityLiveRegion is ever live on this screen. If a second row's 'Sign out' is tapped while the first is still finishing its 'Signed out' acknowledgement, that first removal is finalized right away..."* — same sentence structure, same claim, same order, essentially the same words.
- **Timer-constant comments, same template:** c:39-42 (`REDEEM_ACK_MS`) — *"How long the inline 'Redeemed…' acknowledgement stays visible in a row before the row returns to its normal Redeem control. Fixed and deterministic — not derived from any clock."* vs ActiveSessions:43-46 (`SIGN_OUT_ACK_MS`) — *"How long the inline 'Signed out' acknowledgement stays visible in the row before it's actually removed from the list. Fixed and deterministic — not derived from any clock."*
- **RewardRow.tsx vs SessionRow.tsx — literal StyleSheet key collisions:** `row`, `infoBlock`, `tabularNums`, `confirmBlock`, `confirmText`, `confirmActions`, `cancelButton`, `cancelButtonPressed`, `cancelLabel`, `confirmButton`, `confirmButtonPressed`, `confirmLabel` are **byte-for-byte identical key names** in both files (RewardRow.tsx:137-274 vs SessionRow.tsx:120-223) — 12 of roughly 20 keys. This is exactly the signal GENERATION.md §3's L2 rule names as the primary disqualifier ("스타일 키 이름 집합이... 겹치거나").
- **Doc-comment near-duplication:** RewardRow.tsx:20-23 — *"Deliberately NOT `accessible={true}` anywhere in this file: the row holds its own tappable Redeem / Cancel / Confirm Pressables, and collapsing that subtree into one opaque focus unit would make those controls unreachable to VoiceOver/TalkBack."* vs SessionRow.tsx:11-14 — *"...the row wraps its own tappable 'Sign out' / 'Cancel' / 'Confirm sign out' Pressables, and collapsing that subtree into one opaque focus unit would make those controls unreachable to VoiceOver/TalkBack."*
- **Identical accessibilityHint template:** RewardRow.tsx:68 `"Opens a confirmation for this redemption, in place."` vs SessionRow.tsx:55 `"Opens a confirmation for this session, in place."`

Candidate c's own header comments (PointsRedemptionScreen.tsx:1-31) disclaim only against the Referral/Rewards and Wallet screens — domain-level, and never mention sessions/SessionRow at all, despite being its closest structural relative in the whole catalog.

**Control check — this was avoidable.** `native/src/evolve/r44/b/PickupWindowRow.tsx` explicitly generalizes the *same* SessionRow idiom ("first established as a bottom-band transform, then generalized to a destructive per-row control in sessions/SessionRow.tsx," r44/b:6-7) to a non-destructive, 3-phase persistent-"confirmed" variant — and pointedly renames every single style key (`requestButton`/`pendingPanel`/`cancelAction`/`confirmAction`/`confirmedPanel` vs SessionRow's `signOutButton`/`confirmBlock`/`cancelButton`/`confirmButton`/`signedOutBlock`), stating outright: *"Style keys, copy and the confirmed/'Change' treatment below are original to this screen, not copied from that row."* The catalog already demonstrates the correct way to build on this idiom; candidate c did not follow it.

**Verdict:** a genuine, severe code-surface collision — both identifier/style-key-level and comment/prose-level — with `native/src/sessions/SessionRow.tsx` and `ActiveSessionsScreen.tsx`. The ambient always-visible balance bar above the list is a real (if minor) additional wrinkle not present in ActiveSessionsScreen, but the row-confirm engine underneath is close to a reskin.
