## Candidate B — `product-detail-evolve/r1/b`

**Fenwick Audio** (fictional pro-audio hardware brand) — SKU detail page for the **Aria II**, a bus-powered 2-in/2-out USB-C audio interface. Macro-skeleton: no split-hero/buy-box at all — a compact single-column "overview" band (eyebrow, `h1`, one-liner, rating link, price, finish + bundle selectors, primary CTA, trust row) sits fully visible above the fold, then the page drops into a **full-bleed vertical narrative** (Gallery → Specifications → Compare tiers → Reviews) running down a single content column, paired with a **scroll-spy spec rail** that appears only at `lg+`: an IntersectionObserver-driven side nav that highlights whichever narrative section currently owns the viewport and echoes the live (bundle-linked) price at its foot — proof of value stays anchored in the initial band, not deferred into the rail. Six live interactions: (1) a finish radio-group (Graphite/Silver) that repaints the same deterministic SVG "spec-diagram" renders and updates the ships-in copy, (2) a bundle radio-group (Interface only / +Cables / +Producer Software) that recomputes the price shown in both the hero and the sticky header in lockstep, (3) a gallery with arrow buttons, a thumbnail tablist, and left/right arrow-key support cycling four hand-drawn front/rear/top/desk views, (4) four independently-expandable spec accordion sections (`aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`, first section open by default), (5) a tier-comparison matrix with a "Show differences only" toggle that filters out rows identical across all three tiers while pinning price, and (6) a reviews panel with independent rating-summary bars, a five-way star filter, and a sort control (helpful/newest/highest/lowest) over an `aria-live`-announced result count. Product photography is deliberately replaced by inline, finish-aware SVG renders (no remote images at all) to keep the gallery fully deterministic. Light theme, neutral zinc scale with a single AA-checked burnt-orange accent (`orange-700` for text/fills on white, `orange-600` reserved for icons and large-scale fills), `--font-display-grotesk` on the wordmark and `h1` only, exactly three Tailwind weight classes (`font-normal`/`font-medium`/`font-semibold`) route-wide.
