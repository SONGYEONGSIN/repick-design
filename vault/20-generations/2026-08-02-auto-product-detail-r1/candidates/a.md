## Candidate A — `product-detail-evolve/r1/a`

**Fernway** (peer-to-peer verified-resale marketplace) — single-listing detail page for a Fieldstone Co. Classic Low-Top Sneaker, US-size marketplace SKU. Macro-skeleton: no left/right split and no sticky sidebar buy box — full-width media sits above a horizontal "instrumentation ledger" bar (size selector + live price/condition/ask-count + Add to bag, all resting-state visible without scroll or interaction), followed by a full-width horizontal tab strip stacking Overview / Condition report / Measurements / Seller & shipping / Reviews sections below. Five live interactions: (1) gallery arrow/thumbnail navigation swapping the main photo and its caption, (2) a US-size selector that recomputes price, condition grade, live-ask count and ship window per size (real resale order-book behavior, not a cosmetic swatch), (3) a keyboard-navigable ARIA tablist switching detail sections, (4) an independently-expandable condition-report accordion (four inspection groups, each always showing its numeric score at rest), and (5) a reviews sort control (recent/highest-rated/most-helpful) that reorders an always-visible review list — plus a bonus IntersectionObserver-driven condensed purchase bar that mirrors the ledger's live price once it scrolls out of view. Light theme, neutral zinc scale with a single AA-corrected gold accent (`#A16207`), `--font-display-grotesk` on the h1/wordmark only, exactly three Tailwind weight classes (`font-normal`/`font-medium`/`font-semibold`) route-wide.
